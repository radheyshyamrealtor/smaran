SMARAN Privacy Policy

App: SMARAN, by RadheyShyam · Last updated: 17 October 2026

The short version

SMARAN works fully offline. Your prayers, counts, notes and settings stay on your phone. We do not collect your name, email, location or contacts. We have no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Your favourite prayers, mala counts, progress and streak
  • Your chosen deity, reminder time and sound settings
  • Any intention or note you write in the app

This information is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. If you uninstall the app, it is removed, unless you saved a backup file yourself.

Backups

If you choose "Export backup", the app saves a file to your Downloads folder. You decide where it goes. We never receive it.

Notifications

If you allow them, SMARAN sends one gentle reminder each morning at the time you choose, and a note before festivals and Ekadashis. These are created on your phone, and you can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

Sharing a prayer card

When you tap share, the app makes a picture on your phone and gives it to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp. We do not see what you share or who you share it with.

Purchases

If you buy SMARAN Plus, the payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card details. The store tells the app only whether your purchase is active.

Children

SMARAN does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for any meaningful change, tell you inside the app.
